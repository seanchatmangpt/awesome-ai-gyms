# P63 · Combinatorial Maximalism { #p63 }

> **Family:** [VIII · Self-Manufacturing Ecosystem](../self-manufacturing.md)  
> **Confidence:** ★★★  
> **Canonical ID:** `P63`

## Context

The ecosystem can compose many planners, packs, providers, worlds, policies, and formulations.

## Problem

Humans often collapse the graph early because the full combination space feels intractable, losing potentially superior lawful configurations.

## Forces

- Combinatorics explode quickly.
- Most combinations are invalid.
- Machine-state actuation must remain conservative.

## Resolution

**Preserve the maximal reversible lawful graph in ontology, planning, and construction space; prune with explicit constraints and evidence; delay irreversible selection; and keep all DO transitions behind bounded authority.**

## Consequences

Machines explore what humans cannot enumerate while consequence remains controlled.

## Falsifier

The system discards reversible candidates before a constraint or evidence edge justifies the removal, or lets broad exploration expand ambient actuation authority.
