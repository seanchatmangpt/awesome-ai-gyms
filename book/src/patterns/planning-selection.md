# III · Planning and Selection { #family-planning-selection }

How to search, formulate, compare, and select while keeping plans powerless until admitted.

DFCM preserves multiple planners, formulations, schedules, and candidate actions in reversible space. Selection narrows the graph; it does not grant authority.

## DFCM composition rule

Preserve alternative formulations and partial orders until explicit constraints, evidence, cost, or authority justify pruning. A selected plan remains a powerless intent.
