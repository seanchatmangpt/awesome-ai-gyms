# Glossary

**Admission**  
A pre-actuation decision that an exact subject and candidate transition satisfy declared semantic, authority, policy, and resource constraints.

**ALIVE**  
Scoped standing requiring observed execution against the exact admitted subject and the relevant verifier evidence.

**BRCE**  
Brokered Receipted Consequence Executor: the exclusive DO boundary in this architecture.

**Candidate**  
A discovered subject that may be useful. Candidate status does not imply compatibility or capability.

**Capability**  
A behavior with a declared contract and scoped evidence. In this language, implementation presence alone is insufficient.

**Consequence**  
A machine-state, external-system, persistent, economic, published, or otherwise meaningful state change caused by DO.

**CONSTRUCT**  
Manufacture of artifacts, plans, configs, code, graphs, or intents without inheriting authority to cross the consequence boundary.

**DFCM**  
Design for Combinatorial Maximalism: preserve maximal reversible lawful possibilities before irreversible selection, bounded by ontology, capability, authority, cost, and evidence.

**Gym**  
A bounded executable world plus tasks/scenarios, observation semantics, authority boundary, consequence semantics, verification, and evidence/replay obligations.

**GymAct**  
The world execution system in this ecosystem; it owns admitted observation, DO through BRCE, verification, receipts, and replay.

**ggen**  
The deterministic manufacturing engine that projects admitted semantic graphs through qualified packs into canonical artifacts.

**ggen-create**  
The pack-authoring/reverse-compilation process used to discover reusable manufacturing law and create candidate ggen packs. It is not the consumer-project generator.

**O\***  
Admitted, aligned, grounded, and bounded observation used as lawful manufacturing input.

**Ontology**  
The canonical semantic graph describing stable identities, relationships, capability contracts, and constraints from which projections can be manufactured.

**Policy**  
A behavior configuration such as planner × parameters × objective × observation projection × action projection; distinct from role and agent runtime.

**Receipt**  
A deterministic evidence object binding exact subject, admitted intent, authority, executor, consequence, verifier, and replay references.

**Replay**  
Reconstruction of the admitted subject, world, input, authority, and verifier under a declared equivalence relation.

**SELECT**  
Reversible choice among candidates, plans, formulations, or policies. Selection is powerless until separately admitted and authorized.

**Standing**  
Evidence-bounded status such as UNKNOWN, PARTIAL_ALIVE, ALIVE, BLOCKED, BUILD_BROKEN, UNSUPPORTED, or typed REFUSED.

**World**  
A bounded state and transition system with explicit observation, reset, consequence, and external-dependency semantics.
