# The Generative Sequence

The following sequence is the shortest path through the language for a new gym or gym-enabled project.

1. **Preserve the candidate graph.** Begin with [Possibility Registry](patterns/discovery-composition.md) and [Candidate Is Not Capability](patterns/discovery-composition.md).
2. **Name the exact subject and world.** Apply [Canonical Subject](patterns/foundations.md) and [Bounded World](patterns/foundations.md).
3. **Create machine-checkable semantics.** Apply [Ontology First](patterns/manufacture.md) and [Explicit Observation](patterns/foundations.md).
4. **Fence execution.** Apply [Admission Before Action](patterns/foundations.md), [Authority Boundary](patterns/foundations.md), and [BRCE Is the Only DO](patterns/execution-safety.md).
5. **Explore without accidental consequence.** Apply [Planner League](patterns/planning-selection.md) and [Selection Is Not Authorization](patterns/planning-selection.md).
6. **Manufacture deterministically.** Apply [One Pack, One Manufacturing Boundary](patterns/manufacture.md) and [Deterministic Projection](patterns/manufacture.md).
7. **Prove the behavior.** Apply [Project Gym](patterns/manufacture.md), [Independent Postcondition](patterns/execution-safety.md), and [Exact Subject Binding](patterns/evidence-learning.md).
8. **Close the causal loop.** Apply [Receipt DAG](patterns/evidence-learning.md), [Replay Equivalence](patterns/evidence-learning.md), and [Failure Becomes Law](patterns/evidence-learning.md).
9. **Advance only with evidence.** Apply [Five-by-Seven Maturity](patterns/enterprise-operations.md) and [Minimum Plane Governs](patterns/enterprise-operations.md).
10. **Make the system regenerate itself.** End with [Empty Repo Crown](patterns/self-manufacturing.md) and [Delete and Regenerate](patterns/self-manufacturing.md).

This is not the only valid route. DFCM explicitly preserves alternate paths. It is the default route because it maximizes reversible discovery early and concentrates irreversible consequence behind the smallest possible gate.
